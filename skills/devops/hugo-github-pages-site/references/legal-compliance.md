## Impressum (Legal Notice)
- Vereinsregister: Register court and number (Charlottenburg Berlin typical)
- Geschäftsstelle: Secretariat address format: `℅ Name, Straße PL Ort`
- Vorsitzender: Chairperson name
- Steuer-ID: Tax ID format: `DE123456789`

## DSGVO (GDPR/DSGVO)
- **Data collected**: Contact form entries (name, email, message), member data (name, contact, fees)
- **User rights**: Auskunftsrecht (right to information), Löschungsrecht (right to deletion), Widerspruchsrecht (right to objection)
- **Cookies**: No tracking cookies, only technically necessary cookies for session management
- **Responsible party**: Verein name, info@email address

## Haftungsausschluss (Liability Disclaimer)
- No guarantee for timeliness, correctness, completeness of information
- Use of information at own responsibility
- Particularly for: training times/locations, membership fees/conditions, tournament results/schedules
- Club assumes no liability for direct or indirect damages
