Badminton - FOCUS section

Our League Teams

We currently field 5 league teams for the Berlin-Brandenburg association:

  • VfB Kiefholz I: First league team (highest division)
  • VfB Kiefholz II: Second league team
  • VfB Kiefholz III: Third league team
  • VfB Kiefholz IV: Fourth league team
  • VfB Kiefholz V: Fifth league team (possibly recreational league)

Training Offerings

Regular Training

  • Monday 16:00-17:30 at Hänselstraße 14: Open training
  • Wednesday 16:00-17:30 at Hänselstraße 14: Open training
  • Wednesday 20:00-22:00 at Engelhardstraße 18: Advanced training
  • Saturday 10:30-12:00 at Hänselstraße 14: Family and recreational sport

Coached Units

Occasionally we offer coached units with specialized trainers - current info in the WhatsApp group or via email.

Internal Tournaments and Events

  • Monthly internal tournament: Round-robin against all skill levels
  • Year-end tournament: Christmas and summer tournaments
  • Fixture matches: Friendly matches against other clubs

Youth Department

Children and youth training by arrangement. Specific youth slots (often Saturday mornings). We promote the next generation and offer a safe sport for young people.

Competitive Operations

Participation in: Bezirksliga Berlin-Brandenburg, Pokalwettbewerbe, Interne Vereinsmeisterschaften

Equipment and Facilities

  • Club's own equipment available
  • Loan rackets for beginners (limited availability)
  • Shuttles and nets provided by the club

How to Get Involved in League Play

  1. Attend regular training and show your skills
  2. Express interest in league play to the coaching team
  3. For team selection: Tryouts and matches (typically in summer)
  4. Participation in association registration procedures

Hinweis: Der Ligabetrieb erfordert zusätzliches Engagement: Mannschaftsführung, regelmäßige Spielteilnahme und Einhaltung der Liga-Regeln. Wir freuen uns über Engagement, aber es ist kein Muss, um am Breitensport teilzunehmen.
