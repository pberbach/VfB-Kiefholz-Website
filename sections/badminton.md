# One Club for All Nations

VfB Kiefholz e.V. understands itself as a club for all nations. We have members from over 55 countries, making us one of the most international badminton clubs in Berlin. Our main idea is to offer a possibility to meet and play together and practise this wonderful sport. All nations and kinds of people and age are welcome, as long as they agree with the attitude to be open, friendly and polite to each other.

We are open for beginners as well as for very advanced players. We take part in the Berlin-Brandenburg league competition, currently with 5 league teams. Players with a desire for competition and exciting games against strong opponents will find space in our club as well.

We offer possibilities to play as well as coached lessons for various levels. Besides the league competition we organise internal tournaments and friendly matches against other clubs from time to time.

## Youth Department

We have a youth department that already eagerly trains and plays. In the new hall in the Heinrich-Heine-Strasse 14, training takes place on Monday and Wednesday from 16:00-17:30, as well as often on Saturday from 10:30-12:00.

## Membership

We offer flexible payment options: monthly, quarterly or yearly. All prices can be found under "Membership".

# Badminton - FOCUS section

VfB Kiefholz is one of the largest and most international badminton clubs in Berlin. We offer badminton for all ages and skill levels - from beginners to league players.

## Our League Teams

We currently field 5 league teams for the Berlin-Brandenburg association:

- **VfB Kiefholz I**: First league team (highest division)
- **VfB Kiefholz II**: Second league team
- **VfB Kiefholz III**: Third league team
- **VfB Kiefholz IV**: Fourth league team
- **VfB Kiefholz V**: Fifth league team (possibly recreational league)

## Training Offerings

### Regular Training

- **Monday 16:00-17:30** at Hänselstraße 14: Open training
- **Wednesday 16:00-17:30** at Hänselstraße 14: Open training  
- **Wednesday 20:00-22:00** at Engelhardstraße 18: Advanced training
- **Saturday 10:30-12:00** at Hänselstraße 14: Family and recreational sport

### Coached Units

Occasionally we offer coached units with specialized trainers - current info in the WhatsApp group or via email.

## Internal Tournaments and Events

- **Monthly internal tournament**: Round-robin against all skill levels
- **Year-end tournament**: Christmas and summer tournaments
- **Fixture matches**: Friendly matches against other clubs

## Youth Department

- Children and youth training by arrangement
- Specific youth slots (often Saturday mornings)
- We promote talent and offer a safe sport for young people

## Competitive Operations

Participation in:
- **Bezirksliga Berlin-Brandenburg**
- **Pokalwettbewerbe**
- **Interne Vereinsmeisterschaften**

## Equipment and Facilities

- Club's own equipment available
- Loan rackets for beginners (limited availability)
- Shuttles and nets provided by the club

## How to Get Involved in League Play

1. Attend regular training and show your skills
2. Express interest in league play to the coaching team
3. For team selection: Tryouts and matches (typically in summer)
4. Participation in association registration procedures

---

> **Hinweis:** Der Ligabetrieb erfordert zusätzliches Engagement: Mannschaftsführung, regelmäßige Spielteilnahme und Einhaltung der Liga-Regeln. Wir freuen uns über Engagement, aber es ist kein Muss, um am Breitensport teilzunehmen.
