Cricket

The cricket section of VfB Kiefholz is in very early planning stages. The club has cricket as another sport on offer, but regular training or league operations are not yet established.

Why cricket at VfB Kiefholz?

  • Diversified sports offering: Complement to our main sport, badminton
  • International community: Cricket is popular in many of our members' home countries (over 55 nations in the club)
  • Cultural exchange: Planned opportunity for intercultural sports exchange
  • Club philosophy: Part of the "one club for all nations" philosophy

Placeholder Pricing

Once cricket is regularly established in the club, fees would likely follow a structure similar to badminton:

  • Monthly Adults: approx. 22.50 €
  • Reduced (students, under 30, pensioners over 66): approx. 17.00 €
  • Yearly option: approx. 220 € (with approx. 10% discount)

These are placeholders and will be updated when cricket is introduced.

How It Will Proceed

  1. Express Interest: Email to info@badminton-treptow.de with subject "Cricket Interest"
  2. Decision Making: Member decision at the next annual general meeting
  3. Setup Phase: Develop format and schedule together with interested players
