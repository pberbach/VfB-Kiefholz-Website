## Takeactive Booking Information

**Note**: Court booking is done via the **takeactive** app/website. New members receive access credentials after acceptance. For guests and interested parties: Send an email to info@badminton-treptow.de to request a time slot.

*The halls are used free of charge per Berlin law - no additional hall rental fees are charged.*
