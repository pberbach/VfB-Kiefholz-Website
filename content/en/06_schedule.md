## Important Notes

- **No training on public holidays** and during Berlin-Brandenburg school holidays (except Saturday slots, which often run)
- **Late arrival**: Please arrive on time, the gate closes after start
- **Equipment**: Own racket recommended, some loan rackets available
- **Shoes**: Indoor court shoes mandatory (no street shoes on the court)

## Season and Holiday Schedule

- **Summer break**: Approximately 4 weeks in August (current info via email)
- **Christmas time**: Reduced training schedule (info announced in good time)
- **Bridge days**: Training takes place if enough players are present

## Contact for Arrangement

- For new interested parties: Email to info@badminton-treptow.de
- For club members: Use the Takeactive app
- Current changes: Announced via email and in the WhatsApp group

## Hall Rules

1. Hall shoes must be clean (no road salt, no grit)
2. Balls must be collected from the court
3. Drinks are allowed, but: No glass on the court!
4. Sweep the court after training
