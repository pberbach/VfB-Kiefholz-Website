# Homepage

**One club for all nations**: Badminton in Berlin like nowhere else.

We are an internationally-oriented club. Over 55 nations meet here to play badminton, form friendships, and live intercultural understanding. English is our second language - but everyone is welcome, regardless of origin, age, or skill level.

## What we offer

- **Recreational sport & league**: From recreational play to 5 league teams representing Berlin-Brandenburg
- **Youth department**: Training for children and youth on Monday and Wednesday at Hänselstraße 14
- **Coached sessions**: Coaching for various skill levels
- **Internal tournaments**: Regular friendly matches and tournaments against other clubs

## Current training times

- **Hänselstraße 14**: Monday 16:00-17:30, Wednesday 16:00-17:30, Saturday 10:30-12:00
- **Engelhardstraße 18**: Wednesday 20:00-22:00

## Membership

We offer flexible payment options: monthly, quarterly, or yearly. All prices are under "Membership".

*VfB Kiefholz e.V. was founded in 2004. We understand ourselves as a club for all - regardless of nation, origin, or sporting background. All nations and age groups are welcome, as long as they are open, friendly, and polite to each other.

> **Important**: The halls are used free of charge per Berlin law. Membership fees cover only organizational costs and wear and tear.*
