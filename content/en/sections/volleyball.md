# Volleyball at VfB Kiefholz

## Overview

**Note**: Volleyball has its own dedicated website at [kiefholz.net](https://kiefholz.net) that contains all volleyball-specific information, teams, training times, and match reports.

## VfB Kiefholz Volleyball

The volleyball section of VfB Kiefholz is independently organized and fully represented on the dedicated website.

### What's on the Volleyball Website:

- All league teams (men, women, mixed)
- Training times and locations
- League schedules and results
- Team presentations and photos
- Contact to the volleyball board

### Important Links

- **Volleyball main page**: [kiefholz.net](https://kiefholz.net)
- **Instagram**: [@vfb.kiefholz.volleyball](https://www.instagram.com/vfb.kiefholz.volleyball/)
- **Training venues**: Various Berlin halls (detailed on kiefholz.net)

### Badminton Players Playing Volleyball

VfB Kiefholz members who also play volleyball often find themselves in the recreational volleyball teams ("Himalayan Spikers", "Anime Spikers", "Kiefholz II Mixed"). For volleyball membership inquiries, please contact the volleyball board directly via the main website.
