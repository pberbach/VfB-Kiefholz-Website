# Basketball at VfB Kiefholz

## Introduction

The basketball section of VfB Kiefholz is currently being set up. The club does not currently offer regular basketball training or league operations, but plans to introduce the sport in the near future.

## Current Status

- **Training Offering**: Planned for future introduction
- **League Participation**: Planned for medium-term implementation
- **Contact**: For interest or questions, send an email to info@badminton-treptow.de

## Why Basketball at VfB Kiefholz?

- **Diversified Sports Offering**: Complement to our main sport, badminton
- **International Community**: Open to players of all nations and backgrounds
- **Youth Development**: Planned offerings for young players
- **Club Philosophy**: Part of the "one club for all nations" philosophy

## Price Information (analog to Badminton)

Once basketball is regularly established in the club, fees would likely follow a structure similar to badminton:

- Monthly Adults: approx. 22.50 €
- Reduced (students, under 30, pensioners over 66): approx. 17.00 €
- Yearly option: approx. 220 € (with approx. 10% discount)

*These are placeholders and will be updated when basketball is introduced.*

## How It Will Proceed

1. **Express Interest**: Email to info@badminton-treptow.de with subject "Basketball Interest"
2. **Decision Making**: Member decision at the next annual general meeting
3. **Setup Phase**: Develop training structure and times together with interested players
