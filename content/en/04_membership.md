# Membership at VfB Kiefholz e.V.

## How to Become a Member

1. **Try it out**: Just come by during training times or send an email to info@badminton-treptow.de
2. **Application**: Send a short application email (name, contact, badminton experience, preferred days)
3. **Confirmation**: The board confirms the acceptance via email
4. **Membership Application**: After confirmation, fill out and sign the application

## Fee Structure

We offer flexible payment options:

| Payment Type | Adults (monthly) | Adults (quarterly) | Adults (yearly) |
|--------------|------------------|--------------------|-----------------|
| **Regular** | 22.50 € | 60 € | 220 € |
| **Reduced** (students, under 30, pensioners over 66) | 17.00 € | 45 € | 162 € |
| **Multi-club members** (also member of another badminton club) | 17.00 € | 45 € | 162 € |

### Important Notes:

- **Monthly payment**: Flexible, cancellable monthly (about +10% surcharge)
- **Quarterly payment**: Existing members remain quarterly billed
- **Yearly payment**: Most affordable option (about 10% discount), cancellable once a year

## Injury/Illness Pause

In case of severe illness or injury (more than one month), the contribution period can be paused with a medical certificate. Please contact info@badminton-treptow.de.

## Cancellation Periods

- **Monthly payment**: Cancellable at the end of each quarter
- **Quarterly payment**: Cancellable at the end of the quarter
- **Yearly contribution**: Cancellable at the end of the year

## Membership Application

The formal membership application is available for download. After filling out and signing, send it via email to info@badminton-treptow.de or hand it in at the secretariat.
