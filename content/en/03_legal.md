## Data Protection (GDPR)

This website only collects and uses personal data that is technically necessary. No data is shared with third parties, unless this is necessary for club management.

### Data Collected:

1. **Contact Form**: Those using the contact form consent to the storage of the entered data (name, email, message) purpose-bound. The data is used exclusively for processing the request and deleted after completion.

2. **Member Management**: Member data (name, contact, fees) is stored for club management. This data is treated confidentially according to the club's bylaws.

3. **Cookies**: This website uses no tracking cookies. Only technically necessary cookies for session management are used.

### Your Rights:

- **Right to information**: You can at any time request information about your stored data.
- **Right to deletion**: You can request deletion of your data, unless statutory retention periods stand in the way.
- **Right to objection**: You can object to the data processing.

### Responsible for data processing:

VfB Kiefholz e.V., info@badminton-treptow.de
