Cancellation Periods

  • Monthly Payment: Cancellable at the end of each quarter
  • Quarterly Payment: Cancellable at the end of the quarter
  • Yearly Contribution: Cancellation once a year possible

Injury Pause

In case of severe illness or injury (more than one month), the contribution period can be paused with a medical certificate. Please contact info@badminton-treptow.de.

Membership Application

The formal membership application is available for download. After filling out and signing, send it via email to info@badminton-treptow.de or hand it in at the secretary.
