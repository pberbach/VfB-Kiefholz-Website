Membership

How to Become a Member

  1. Schnuppern: Kommen Sie einfach zu den Trainingszeiten vorbei oder schreiben Sie eine E-Mail an info@badminton-treptow.de
  2. Bewerbung: Senden Sie eine kurze Vorstellung per E-Mail (Name, Kontakt, Badminton-Erfahrung, bevorzugte Tage)
  3. Bestätigung: Das Vorstandsteam bestätigt die Aufnahme per E-Mail
  4. Beitrittserklärung: Nach Bestätigung ausgefüllt unterschreiben und abgeben

Important Notes

  • Monthly Payment: Flexible, monthly cancellable (about +10% surcharge)
  • Quarterly Payment: Existing members remain quarterly billed
  • Yearly Contribution: Cheapest option (- about 10% discount), cancellation once a year possible

Injury Pause

In case of severe illness or injury (more than one month), the contribution period can be paused with a medical certificate. Please contact info@badminton-treptow.de.

Cancellation Periods

  • Monthly Payment: Cancellable at the end of each quarter
  • Quarterly Payment: Cancellable at the end of the quarter
  • Yearly Contribution: Cancellation once a year possible

Membership Application

The formal membership application is available for download. After filling out and signing, send it via email to info@badminton-treptow.de or hand it in at the secretary.
